Cook from what you save, not what you scroll.

Pomegranate pulls recipes out of any web page or screenshot and keeps them all in one place. Plan a menu from your recipes and get a shopping list in a few taps. No scrolling past someone’s life story to find the ingredients.

What it does

Three things, done properly

Import

Import from anywhere

Paste the link to a recipe page, or add a screenshot of a recipe from social media or a cookbook. Pomegranate pulls out just the ingredients and steps. Nothing else.

Plan & shop

Menus to shopping lists

Pick recipes for the week and Pomegranate builds one shopping list, grouped by aisle. Share it with your partner to do the shop, or work from the same list together and tick things off as you go.

Cook

Cook with what you have

Search your saved recipes by ingredient to see what you can make with what’s in the fridge. Mark the winners Tried & Tested so they’re easy to find next time.

The name

Why Pomegranate?

A splash in every dish

Even a spoonful of arils adds a burst of color and tart-sweet flavor to almost anything you’re cooking, the same way one good recipe can lift a whole week of meals.

Jewels, not just seeds

Cut one open and you’ll find hundreds of glistening arils packed together. Every recipe you save here is a little jewel too, worth keeping and worth coming back to.

It comes with a crown

Look closely and every pomegranate wears its own little crown. We think the recipes you treasure deserve the same royal treatment.

Cooking better starts with saving better.
