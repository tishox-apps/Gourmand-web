Delete your Pomegranate account

Pomegranate is developed by tishox. This page explains how to permanently delete your account and data, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — From within the app

  1. Open Pomegranate and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately — there is no waiting period.

Option 2 — If you no longer have the app installed

Email us from the address associated with your account and request deletion:

tishox.apps@gmail.com

We will delete your account and data within 7 days of receiving your request.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email address, cuisine preferences)
  • All recipes you've created, including ingredients, steps and tags
  • All shopping lists you've created
  • All photos you've uploaded
  • Your account sign-in itself

Deletion is permanent and immediate — nothing is kept, and there is no retention period or recovery window after a request is completed.
