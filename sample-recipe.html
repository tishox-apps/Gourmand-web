Thai Basil Pork Stir-Fry

Serves 4 · Ready in 25 minutes · Thai-inspired

A loud, fast, fragrant stir-fry — pork mince cooked hard with garlic and chilli, finished with a fish-sauce-and-oyster-sauce glaze and a big handful of Thai basil stirred through right at the end so it stays bright rather than wilting into the pan. Good over rice on a night when you want dinner on the table in well under half an hour.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 small red chillies, sliced (less or more to taste)
  • 500 g pork mince
  • 1 brown onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves, loosely packed
  • Steamed jasmine rice, to serve
  • 4 eggs, fried (optional, for serving)

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat.
  2. Add the garlic and chilli and stir-fry for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  3. Add the pork mince and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned all over — about 4-5 minutes.
  4. Add the onion and capsicum and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes, until just softened.
  5. Stir in the fish sauce, soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar, and cook for another minute.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir through the Thai basil leaves until just wilted.
  7. Serve immediately over steamed rice, topped with a fried egg if you like.
