Teriyaki Salmon Bites Bowl

Main · Mediterranean · Quick

Ingredients

  • 6 salmon fillets
  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • White sesame seeds
  • Teriyaki sauce
  • 2 heads broccoli

Method

  1. Cut the salmon into bite-sized cubes.
  2. Marinate the salmon in teriyaki sauce.
  3. Pan-fry the salmon, broccoli and spring onions in butter.
  4. Serve over rice with a lemon wedge, and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
